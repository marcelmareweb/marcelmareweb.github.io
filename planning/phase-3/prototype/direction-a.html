A FIELD OF COMMITMENTS / FICTIONAL ORGANISATION
Solid = backed · dashed = deferredOpen questions remain.
Back up to two priorities.

For this fictional organisation, capacity allows two commitments. Clear a choice to revise it.

Read the worked example and its trade-offs

Backing another market and closer customer relationships requires customer evidence, delivery capability and service capacity. Both draw on leadership attention; choosing both leaves that conflict unresolved. Reliability and AI workflow changes are deferred for now.

Before acting, investigate the market evidence, the customers whose needs matter and whether the organisation has capacity. A different combination creates different obligations. There is no correct formation.

Fictional design exercise. The two-commitment limit and relationships are authored examples. This is not a diagnostic, assessment or strategic advice. No information is saved or sent.

A / Editorial Monumentalism

Creative rationale

Make judgement feel consequential. Typographic mass and deliberate emptiness create confidence before detail.

Colour

Obsidian field, acid-yellow choice movement, cream text. Contrast separates the editorial movements.

Typography

Archivo 800 display; Archivo 400–600 interface and reading. Tight display leading, comfortable prose.

Layout

Full-width headline; strict horizontal rules; large evidence panels. Mobile restores a simple reading order.

Interaction

A commitment fills its alternative; solid relationships expose dependencies; exclusions retain dashed outlines.

Motion

160ms control feedback, 240ms relationship emphasis. No entrance sequence, looping or automatic convergence.

Risks and trade-offs

May feel severe. Long uppercase statements need careful mobile scale. Yellow must remain selective.

Review scope: six homepage movements, capability treatment, representative project and a working fictional interaction. Secondary destinations are retained in the planning documents; this is not the rebuilt production site.
