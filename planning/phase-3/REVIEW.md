## Graphic grammar

The field uses typographic commitment slips, open brackets, a finite capacity rail and labelled routes. It is an authored editorial composition, not a force-directed graph. There are no particles, glowing nodes, neurons or ambient movement. The arrangement is deliberately asymmetric, and its shared constraint is made explicit.

- A bounded slip represents an alternative.
- A filled slip and the word BACKED represent commitment.
- A dashed slip and the word DEFERRED preserve an exclusion.
- A labelled route identifies a dependency.
- A bounded rail represents finite capacity, with a stronger dashed treatment when a shared demand needs examination.
- Open brackets mark assumptions and consequences that remain available for scrutiny.

The exact monkey files are copied from `public/logo.png` and `public/logo3.png`. They are not recoloured, redrawn, cropped or replaced. Direction C rotates the surrounding image frame as a compositional study; the artwork pixels and proportions remain unchanged.

Open `prototype/typography.html`. The three specimens repeat identical headline, explanatory copy, CTA, logo and reading text, with common specimen sizes to compare font roles rather than allowing layout differences to conceal them.

1. Archivo for display, interface and reading.
2. Archivo display/interface, Newsreader long-form reading.
3. Current font-role control: Newsreader headings, Plus Jakarta Sans body/interface and JetBrains Mono labels. This preserves the existing roles, not every current-site spacing value.

Assess the mobile and desktop specimens together. Archivo supplies a direct grotesk voice compatible with the strong monkey mark; Newsreader creates a useful change of reading register. Neither preference establishes readability for all readers. Browser measurements, font bytes and test limitations are recorded in `QA_REPORT.md`.

Provisional recommendation: Archivo commercial interface and headings. Keep Newsreader as a candidate for long-form editorial reading pending approval and a smaller production font subset. No production font is changed.
