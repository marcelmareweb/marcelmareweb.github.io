One consequential pause belongs to the finished-loaf inspection gate while upstream processes continue. There are no A/B routes, game mechanics or explanatory labels inside the artwork.

No yellow or other authored colour appears in the current illustration. Existing coloured brand assets and website palette remain unchanged. Earlier coloured concepts are archived for comparison only.
