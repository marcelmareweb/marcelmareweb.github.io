# Latest Stage 1 revision: complete editorial redesign

The previous mechanical refinement is superseded for review by a new hand-drawn art direction. Its files are preserved in `engineered-monochrome-version/`; earlier archives remain intact.

Open [the new illustration review](index.html), [the comparison](comparison.html), [the desktop display specimen](desktop-specimen.html) or [the mobile display specimen](mobile-specimen.html).

The new composition climbs to an elevated peeler, suspends a skin basket, drives a low bowl from overhead pulleys and leads to a crooked-chimney oven. Fine irregular ink, uneven trestles and selective hatching replace the geometric diagram language. Yellow appears only on banana skins. Tiny monkeys supervise; the final loaf waits behind a physical stop until its inspector operates the lever.

## Decisions awaiting approval

1. The substantially different hand-drawn illustration language and asymmetric mechanical world.
2. Yellow skins as the only colour, with white fruit, batter and loaf.
3. Approximately 4–5% worker height relative to desktop scene width, and the small mobile inspector.
4. The mobile recomposition and legibility at actual display sizes.
5. The 14-second story, including the two-second inspection hold and lever-controlled release.

These are static raster design specimens, not production animation assets. Preparing articulated/vector artwork and validating the moving mechanisms belong to Stage 2 after approval. The homepage, original logos, source files and production deployment are unchanged.
