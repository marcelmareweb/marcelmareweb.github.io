# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Senior leaders and teams in organisations facing consequential, uncertain decisions: strategy, policy, behaviour change and AI adoption. They arrive with a live question and need to judge whether this practice can help them examine it.

## Product Purpose

The Spotted Ape (Pty) Ltd is an independent strategic decision and behavioural design practice in Cape Town, South Africa. The site explains how the practice helps, shows its evidence and thinking, and leads visitors into an enquiry or a workshop/learning option. Success is a qualified enquiry through `/contact/` or a visitor choosing a fitting workshop or course.

## Positioning

Helps leaders make consequential choices when familiar assumptions no longer hold, by examining alternatives, assumptions and trade-offs (Strategic Decision Design), the incentives and conditions that shape action (Behavioural Design), and decision authority and accountability where AI is involved (Human–AI Decision Design). AI is one relevant context, not the identity of the business.

## Operating Context

Enquiries go through `/contact/` (name, email and question required; offering context carried by `contactHref`). Workshops, the five-day Strategic Design Leadership in the Age of AI masterclass (E–P–I–A–S, scoped to its own route), applied courses, research and advisory conversations support the practice. Research, articles and `/Courses/` archives stay on thespottedape.com as external links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static Astro site, published only at https://marcelmareweb.github.io. No public cohort dates, seat prices, guarantees, cart or checkout. The Find Formation exercise keeps its two-choice limit and no-data-sent promise. See AGENTS.md for the full rules, which take precedence.

## Brand Commitments

Name and wordmark: THE SPOTTED APE, on one line. The ape identity and decision instruments are preserved. Voice: short, confident, period-ended statements. Retired and not to be reintroduced: the AI-agency positioning, !Kharos Strategy, the Agentic Mastery framework.

## Evidence on Hand

- Case account: policy analyst strategic review (`src/content/cases/strategic-review.md`).
- Behavioural research: cookstove adoption in South Africa (qualified findings).
- Executive masterclass (`src/data/executive-masterclass.ts`), Solar learning case (`/courses`), strategic decision maps (`src/data/strategicMatrices.ts`), Judgement Factory (`public/images/judgement-factory/`).
- Insights from the Substack feed.

Absent, and not to be fabricated: client names or logos, testimonials, metrics, press, photography and video.

## Product Principles

- Show the reasoning, not claims: alternatives, assumptions, trade-offs and real evidence.
- Keep findings qualified; never overstate outcomes or what AI can establish.
- Keep every route to an enquiry honest and low-friction.
- Essential content works without animation or JavaScript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion and no-JavaScript access to essential content are required.
