# Blueprint assessment

Audit date: 30 October 2026. This is a strategic interpretation of the supplied workshop sales-page document, not an approved offer or implementation specification.

Source: “Here is a complete, conversion-focused wireframe and copy blueprint…” supplied in the attachment ending f2f3a099-903f-42bc-811c-09c864afeff4/Pasted text.txt.

## 1. What the document is designed to do

The document addresses two purchase situations for one workshop: a person ready to reserve a place, and an organisational buyer arranging a customised team engagement. It combines early practical information, tangible outputs, a syllabus, repeated next steps and a team-enquiry alternative.

That is a useful journey principle. It does not establish that The Spotted Ape sells public workshop seats, operates an academy, offers the example brand-strategy sprint, or needs a cart.

The current local site supports strategic and behavioural work, bespoke workshops, an executive masterclass, a self-paced solar-investment learning case and research/thinking resources. Its next actions include opening learning material, downloading resources and enquiring about engagements. Preserve these differences.

## 3. Inconsistencies and omissions in the supplied document

1. The overview names eight sections, while the detailed document has six differently ordered sections. A final page structure has not been resolved.
2. “Who should attend & prerequisites”, facilitator authority/proof, and FAQ/guarantee have no complete corresponding detailed sections. The guarantee appears as hero microcopy without an explanation of its conditions.
3. The overview puts the corporate banner sixth; the detailed version puts it fourth. This matters to how much an organisational buyer must read before finding a relevant route.
4. The enquiry is described as four fields but displays five: name, email, company, team size and goals. The role of optional fields is not resolved.
5. Four 90-minute modules total six teaching hours. Adding two 15-minute breaks produces 6.5 hours, while the headline and time windows imply seven hours. Fifteen minutes per day remain unallocated.
6. The November hero does not give an individual a clear answer about prerequisites, facilitator, recordings/access, accessibility requirements or the next step after payment.
7. Cohort inventory, invoices, card payments, confirmations, cancellations and team-proposal handling are assumed rather than specified.
8. “Discovery call” implies a scheduling route, but no scheduling interaction or operational arrangement is provided.
9. Two prominent hero CTAs do not by themselves establish a usable mobile hierarchy. Their labels, order and landing states require rendered testing.
10. This is a workshop page outline, not a site-wide architecture. It does not explain where advisory engagements, behavioural research, existing case evidence or free-access learning belong.

These are document observations, not conclusions about customer behaviour.

## 4. What transfers across the practice

Use the sequence **recognise the problem → understand the relevant support → see evidence and outputs → choose an appropriate next step**.

For custom strategic or behavioural support, the next step is a conversation with enough context to be useful. For existing self-paced learning, the next step can be opening the material. For the executive programme, the next step is currently exploring a day or discussing an engagement. These should not be presented as equivalent purchase buttons.

Keep the blueprint’s emphasis on decision-relevant information. Express it through the current editorial system rather than reproducing its e-commerce layout.

## 5. Business facts still needed

- Whether public individual attendance is offered, or whether programmes are commissioned by organisations.
- Whether a person may express interest individually and how that interest will be handled.
- Confirmed delivery modes, locations, day lengths, available dates and capacity.
- Whether any published price is appropriate, including the applicable currency and what it covers.
- Approved terms for cancellations, substitutions, refunds and post-programme access.
- Facilitator availability, any additional facilitators and permission to use specific proof.
- Whether follow-up coaching and implementation support are included, optional or unavailable.
- Which destination should be the primary host for the solar learning case.

These are follow-up business decisions for a future implementation phase. They do not block this strategy.
