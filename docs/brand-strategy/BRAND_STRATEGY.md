# Brand strategy — The Spotted Ape

**Status:** Version 2.2, owner decisions recorded · **Date:** 6 November 2026 · **Owner:** Marcel Maré

This document sets out the brand strategy for The Spotted Ape (Pty) Ltd. It builds on the positioning in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and the visual direction in [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md#design-direction-the-decision-record). It does not replace either: they remain the authority for positioning, design system and site rules.

Version 2.2 records the owner's decisions on the seven open questions from version 1 (Section 12). The main change: **the decision record is now a signature method, not the central brand idea.** The brand idea is *helping teams find formation*, taken from the story behind the name, and the yellow Spotted Ape logo is the key brand asset.

**Evidence key.** Every claim carries one of these tags:

- **[Decided]**: approved by the owner on 6 November 2026.
- **[Supplied]**: stated in the repository, the site or `../the-spotted-ape-sources/`.
- **[Supplied, memo]**: stated in the owner's decision memo of 6 November 2026.
- **[Supplied, essay]** / **[Supplied, case]**: stated in the Flying Spotted Ass Ape essay (November 2025) or the Creative x AI Workshop Series account. Both are now in `../the-spotted-ape-sources/` (`essays/` and `cases/creative-x-ai/`).
- **[Observed]**: seen in the built site on 6 November 2026.
- **[Hypothesis]**: a reasoned proposal that still needs testing.
- **[Unresolved]**: needs permission, verification or agreement before it can be published or acted on.

No client interviews, competitor research, trademark searches, archival checks or recognition tests have been carried out yet. Section 11 sets out the research.

## 1. The strategy on one page

**Brand idea.** *Helping teams find formation.* Like the assembly ship it is named after, The Spotted Ape is a visible point of orientation when conditions are confusing: it helps a team find its formation, then leaves it able to hold that formation without the guide. [Decided, 6 November 2026]

**Principle.** *Useful, never indispensable.* [Decided; Supplied, memo]

**Positioning.** *The Spotted Ape is an independent strategic decision and behavioural design practice helping leaders navigate uncertainty, make consequential choices and create the conditions for purposeful action.* [Decided]

**Capabilities.** The three capabilities are three parts of that proposition: [Decided]

- **Strategic Decision Design:** deciding where to go.
- **Behavioural Design:** understanding what enables people to act.
- **Human–AI Decision Design:** deciding where technology assists and where human judgement remains accountable.

**Lines.** The existing lines are retained. [Decided]

- Master line: *More possibilities. Harder choices.*
- Promise: *See differently. Choose deliberately. Turn the choice into action.*

**Signature method.** *The decision record:* question, alternatives, assumptions, trade-offs, owner and review. It shapes workshop materials, proposals and the site's structure. It is how the practice works, not why a client calls. [Decided]

**Key asset.** *The yellow Spotted Ape logo:* the yellow ape face on a black circle (`public/logo.png`). It is the practice's own assembly ship: the mark a team looks for to form up. [Decided, 6 November 2026: the logo, not the ring-and-spot favicon]

**Proof.** Four pieces of evidence, each labelled with its limits:

- the cookstove study (peer-reviewed behavioural research);
- the Creative x AI workshops (human–AI learning and behavioural design) [Unresolved: permissions];
- Should We Invest in Solar? (an educational simulation);
- *not* the policy-analyst story: it is a fictional teaching scenario (owner, 7 November 2026), labelled as such on its page and used nowhere as evidence.

**What it will not be.** An AI agency, an automation-maturity framework, a generic innovation studio, a source of guaranteed outcomes, or a guide that makes clients dependent on it. [Supplied/Decided]

### 2.2 The most consequential brand problem

**The practice has a coherent idea but presents a catalogue.** [Hypothesis]

The cookstove bakery, the Creative x AI workshops, the Solar course and the decision work look like unrelated projects. What joins them is one way of working: each creates the conditions for people to see differently, choose deliberately and then act on their own.

Without that idea in view, visitors meet three parallel capabilities. They compare each one with a larger specialist (a strategy consultant, a behavioural insights unit, an AI adviser), and on any single axis the practice looks smaller.

**Strategic response.** Lead with helping teams find formation. Tell the story of the name. Present the capabilities and the evidence as different expressions of one principle. [Decided]

## 4. Strategic choices

### 4.1 Primary audience and buying situations

[Hypothesis, to be tested in interviews]

1. **The leader before a commitment.** Several credible priorities and one budget. *Trigger:* a strategy review, a board deadline or a stalled planning cycle.
2. **The team whose agreement has not become action.** *Trigger:* an adoption or uptake problem.
3. **The organisation bringing AI into consequential work.** *Trigger:* a pilot moving into a live workflow, or people unsure what remains theirs to judge.
4. **The learner or the learning buyer.** *Trigger:* a programme brief, or the search for a practical, participatory format.

Situations 1 to 3 are the core. Situation 4 builds reach and reputation, and the evidence for it is now the strongest (cookstove, Creative x AI, Solar).

### 4.2 The customer's job

- **External problem:** too many credible options, and plans that do not survive contact with how people work.
- **Internal problem:** fear of committing to the wrong thing; the sense of losing one's footing, or one's role, as AI enters the work.
- **Philosophical problem:** people should be able to find their own direction and own their choices, not borrow them from an adviser or a tool.

[Hypothesis]

### 4.3 Positioning statement

> The Spotted Ape is an independent strategic decision and behavioural design practice helping leaders navigate uncertainty, make consequential choices and create the conditions for purposeful action. [Decided]

**Onliness test.** *The only practice that gives leaders a point of orientation in uncertainty and leaves them able to proceed without it, joining strategic decision design with participatory behavioural research.*

The word “only” is not substantiated. Use this sentence internally to sharpen focus, never in public copy. [Hypothesis]

**Customer transformation.** *From* confusion among competing priorities and unfamiliar conditions, *to* a direction the team has chosen and can pursue on its own, *through* a guide who orients, builds judgement and then steps back.

## 6. Narrative

### 6.1 The story of the name

[Decided; Supplied, essay; independently checked 7 November 2026, see sources below]

From late 1943, the US Eighth Air Force grew from a few hundred bombers to formations of more than a thousand. Crews took off from dozens of airfields in East Anglia, often in fog and low light, and had to find each other and form up. Each bomb group used an *assembly ship*: a war-weary aircraft painted in loud colours and fitted with flares. It climbed first, circled a fixed point and signalled until its group had formed, then dropped away and returned to base.

The 458th Bombardment Group, at RAF Horsham St Faith near Norwich, had two assembly ships. The first, a polka-dotted B-24D called *First Sergeant*, was destroyed in a flare accident on the runway on 27 May 1944. Its replacement was B-24H-10-DT **41-28697**, which had flown eight combat missions as *Dixie Bell II* between March and May 1944. Repainted in the same scheme, it became the *Spotted Ape* (also *Spotted Ass Ape* and *Wonder Bread*). It carried coloured flares and a lit fuselage band, and flew 69 assembly missions, from 12 June 1944 to 9 March 1945, when a brake failure on landing at Horsham wrote it off. At least 59 pilots from the group's four squadrons flew it.

**Checked facts and corrections to the essay** (7 November 2026):

| Fact | Essay | Independent sources | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Serial | 42-50346 | 41-28697 (B-24H-10-DT) | **Essay wrong**: correct before publishing |
| Group and base | 458th BG, Horsham St Faith | Same | Confirmed |
| Nicknames | *Flying Spotted Ass Ape*, *Spotted Ape* | *Spotted Ape*, *Spotted Ass Ape*, *Wonder Bread*; earlier *Dixie Bell II* | Confirmed; “Flying” not found |
| Period | “From 1943”; “nearly a year” | Assembly duty 12 June 1944 to 9 March 1945 (about nine months, 69 missions) | **Essay overstated** |
| Paint | Polka dots | Same scheme as *First Sergeant* (polka dots); red circle with a lit white band | Confirmed |
| Practice | Climbed first, circled, fired flares, returned home | Stripped bombers with extra flares, a colour per group; formed the group, then returned to base | Confirmed as the general practice |

Sources: [458th Bomb Group association: the Spotted Ape](https://458bg.com/stories/spotted-ape-458th-assembly-ship/); [American Air Museum: 41-28697](https://americanairmuseum.com/aircraft/20148?page=2); [Wikipedia: Assembly ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_ship). Wikipedia also records one occasion when *Spotted Ass Ape* stayed with its formation to the target rather than turning back. That is an exception; do not build the story on “always”.

**The essay's photograph** (traced 7 November 2026). It is based on a wartime press photograph that the 458th Bomb Group association files as the *Spotted Ape* ([458bg.com](https://458bg.com/stories/spotted-ape-458th-assembly-ship/), file `ST-SpottedApe-MajJohnHensler.jpg`). The press caption calls it “THE SHOW OFF SHIP” and names Major John A. Hensler, a squadron commander, walking away from the aircraft, “used as a ‘formation’ plane”. The owner's version was colourised **and extended** with AI: the sky, apron, background bomber and figure are regenerated. Its colours are also wrong: the association's colour photographs show red and black spots and a red mouth, not black spots only.

The essay draws three qualities from it:

- **Example:** assembly ships did not command; they demonstrated. Formation follows behaviour, not memos.
- **Experience:** the aircraft was flown by veterans with tacit knowledge (Polanyi) of the craft and the conditions.
- **Exit:** once the formation held, it turned home. Leadership that withdraws at the right moment leaves competence behind; leadership that lingers breeds dependence.

**Accuracy rules.**

- Use the checked facts above. Name the serial only if needed, and then as 41-28697.
- Present assembly ships as a practice, with this aircraft as one example. Do not suggest that one aircraft solved assembly for the whole Eighth Air Force.
- Tell it as a story of orientation and coordination. Leave out the essay's combat framing (“Judas Goat”, “the slaughter”) and the Ploiești loss figures.
- Do not repeat the essay's claim that early mentorship “can halve misalignment issues” as a practice claim; it breaks the rule against unsourced or overextended figures.

## 7. Verbal identity

### 7.1 Voice

| Trait | Means | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plain** | Short sentences, everyday words, one idea per sentence. “Several priorities. One commitment.” | Simplistic |
| **Exact** | Says what was observed and what was not. Labels evidence by type. | Hedged into vagueness |
| **Questioning** | Leads with the question that matters. “What needs deciding?” | Coy |
| **Calm** | Unhurried, with no urgency tricks and no superlatives. | Cold or academic |
| **Enabling** | Speaks to the reader's own capacity: “you decide”, “your team can”. Credits participants and collaborators. | Self-congratulatory, or positioning the guide as the hero |

**Writing rules.**

- Headlines are short, end in a period and break deliberately. [Supplied]
- Use sentence case; British spelling; round quotes; the en dash in “Human–AI”.
- Name the evidence type beside the claim.
- Use the reader's language: direction, choice, commitment, trade-off, assumption, owner, review.

**Banned words and moves.** Unlock, empower, leverage, transform (unqualified), cutting-edge, innovative, world-class, AI-powered, synergy, “only”, “best”, “guaranteed”, unsourced percentages, fake urgency, and war metaphors used for business (*mission*, *battle*, *target*), except within the historical story itself.

- **One sentence:** *The Spotted Ape helps leaders navigate uncertainty, make consequential choices and create the conditions for purposeful action.* [Decided]

We help leaders examine the choice, test the assumptions behind it and design for what people will actually do, including where AI fits. ### 8.1 The logo is the signature

[Decided, 6 November 2026]

**The yellow Spotted Ape logo**, the yellow ape face on a black circle (`public/logo.png`), is the key brand asset. It plays the part the painted aircraft played: a mark loud enough to be found in a crowded field, around which a team forms up. Every other visual asset supports it; none competes with it. The ring-and-spot favicon is not a brand asset and should not be extended to other uses.

| Element | Meaning | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Spotted Ape logo** | *Orientation and recognition*: the visible reference point | Header and footer (now); favicon, avatars, slide corner, social cards, proposals, workshop materials, email signature |
| **Yellow signal states** (choice control, current page, focus) | *A decision or commitment* | Interactive instruments only |

The logo is a fixed mark and the only place where yellow fills a large shape. Everywhere else, yellow appears only in response to an action. This keeps the two meanings apart.

**Logo rules.**

- Use the artwork as supplied: yellow face, black features and circle. Do not redraw, recolour, outline, rotate, crop or add effects.
- Pair it with the wordmark at 10px spacing for 30px (the header lock-up). On its own, use it where the name already appears nearby (favicon, avatar, slide corner).
- **On ink grounds** the black circle merges with the background, leaving the face. That is acceptable; do not add a white ring.
- **Minimum size:** 16px for the favicon. Below about 24px, check that the eyes and smile remain distinct. Otherwise use 30px or larger.
- **Clear space:** at least a quarter of the logo's width on every side.
- **Files to produce:** a vector master (SVG), a 512px PNG, and an ICO/SVG favicon made from the logo. The current master is an 895 × 809 PNG. [Unresolved: vector source]

## 11. Research and measurement

[Decided. These are exploratory studies, not statistically conclusive experiments. There are no baselines yet.]

### 11.1 Buyer interviews (first)

Interview about eight people: past clients, prospective executive buyers and potential learning buyers. The aim is to establish whether the differentiation matches problems buyers recognise and will pay to solve.

- What was the last consequential decision your organisation struggled to make?
- What made the decision difficult?
- What external help did you consider, and why?
- What would make a specialist advisory engagement worthwhile?
- What would you expect to leave with?
- Would you buy facilitated learning, independent advice or both?

Do not lead respondents towards the words *decision record*, *formation*, *orientation* or *behavioural design*. Their own language is the finding, and it should inform the copy for the next test.

### 11.2 Five-second tests (second)

Test the homepage with about eight people unfamiliar with the practice. After five seconds, ask what The Spotted Ape does, who it serves and what they would expect to find next.

| Variant | Emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| A | *More possibilities. Harder choices.* with the current supporting sentence |
| B | The same headline, with a supporting sentence that names the client problem in the interviewees' words |

Keep the design constant so that differences come from the copy.

### 11.3 Recognition (later)

Measure the fame and uniqueness of the ape logo and the formation motif only after they have been used consistently. Six months is a review point; recognition may take considerably longer to develop.
