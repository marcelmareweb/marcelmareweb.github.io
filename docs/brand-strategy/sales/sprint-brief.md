# Strategic Choices Sprint: brief for a prospect

**Status:** for days 11–14 · **Date:** 8 November 2026 · **Source:** strategy 12.3 (scope confirmed and pilot terms adopted 8 November 2026) · **Worked example:** `../the-spotted-ape-sources/buyer-example/fictional-sprint-brief.md` (fiction; never send it)

Send this after a conversation, never cold, and only when the prospect has said yes to a note. Fill in the bracketed parts from what the prospect said. Fees and timing are never published on the site; they appear only in this brief.

---

**[Business, described without naming it if they prefer]: one decision, properly made**

**The decision.** [The decision in their words, for example "whether to open a second site or invest in the first".] It needs settling by [their date].

**Why it's hard.** [Their words from the conversation: competing options, a team that agrees but doesn't move, an unfamiliar factor such as AI.]

**What we'll do**

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Start | A 60-minute conversation with you, the person who owns the decision |
| Listen | Up to six 45-minute interviews with the people the decision affects |
| Read | Up to five documents that bear on the choice |
| Decide | A one-day facilitated decision workshop with your leadership team |
| Keep | A concise decision record and the agreed follow-through |
| Review | One follow-up conversation to check the decision is holding |

**What you'll leave with.** A choice, the reasons for it, what you've agreed to give up, who owns it, and what would make you look again, all written down so the team can act on it without us. If the evidence doesn't yet support a responsible choice, we say what's missing and what to do next. Here's a sample, made from a fictional teaching case: https://marcelmareweb.github.io/decision-record/

**If the question turns out to be the wrong one.** If the interviews show that the original question is not the one the team needs to resolve, we explain why and agree a revised question with you before the workshop. If that changes the scope, fee or timing, we agree those changes before continuing.

**Timing.** Three weeks from our first conversation, including the review. The workshop is in person at your premises, around day 9; the record follows within three working days; the review is online at the end of week three. Our conversation and the interviews are online. Proposed dates: [first conversation], [workshop], [review].

**Your team's time.** You: the first conversation, the workshop and the review. [4–6, at most 8] leadership participants for the workshop day. Up to six people for a 45-minute interview each.

**Fee.** R45 000, fixed, excluding VAT if applicable. [Travel or an outside venue, only if agreed: amount.] It covers one question, the interviews, up to five documents (about 100 pages in all), the workshop for up to eight people, the record with one round of factual corrections, and the review. It doesn't cover [the specialist work they already have or need: engineering, legal, financial modelling, market research].

**How we work.** Useful, never indispensable. The aim is a team that can make the next hard decision without us.

Marcel Maré · The Spotted Ape · hello@thespottedape.com

## Covering note

> Thank you. As you asked, here's a short note on the decision, the work involved, the time it needs from your team and the fee. Please check I've understood the question before you consider the approach. It doesn't commit you to anything.

## Before sending, check

- [ ] They asked for, or agreed to, a note. A note is permission to send, not a purchase.
- [ ] Only the prospect's own words describe their problem; no names of other clients.
- [ ] The person receiving it can commission the work, or has said who can and how they'll pass it on.
- [ ] Their deadline leaves three weeks, or the schedule is back-planned to fit (a board pack is due before the board meets).
- [ ] The specialist work they need first (finance, engineering, legal) is named as outside the fee.
- [ ] A gap between their budget and the fee is stated plainly, not discounted away.
- [ ] Any partner role (Raykis or other) is agreed before this fee is used.
- [ ] The sample record link works.

## Don't send it when

- they've said they don't want a proposal;
- the missing input is evidence (customer research, a financial model), not a choice;
- the owner already knows the choice and the problem is keeping to it;
- nobody they can reach can commission the work.

Say so instead, and suggest the proportionate next step.
