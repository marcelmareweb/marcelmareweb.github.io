# Strategic Choices Sprint: brief for a prospect

**Status:** draft for days 11–14 · **Date:** 8 November 2026 · **Source:** strategy 12.3 (scope confirmed 8 November 2026)

Send this after a conversation, never cold. Fill in the bracketed parts from what the prospect said. Fees and timing aren't set publicly, so state them per prospect.

---

**[Business name]: one decision, properly made**

**The decision.** [The decision in their words, for example "whether to open a second site or invest in the first".] It needs settling by [date].

**Why it's hard.** [Their words from the conversation: competing options, a team that agrees but doesn't move, an unfamiliar factor such as AI.]

**What we'll do**

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Start | A 60-minute conversation with you, the person who owns the decision |
| Listen | Up to six interviews with the people the decision affects |
| Read | Up to five documents that bear on the choice |
| Decide | A one-day facilitated decision workshop with your leadership team |
| Keep | A concise decision record and the agreed follow-through |
| Review | One follow-up conversation to check the decision is holding |

**What you'll leave with.** A choice, the reasons for it, what you've agreed to give up, who owns it, and what would make you look again, all written down so the team can act on it without us. Here's a sample, made from a fictional teaching case: https://marcelmareweb.github.io/decision-record/

**If the question turns out to be the wrong one.** We say so after the interviews, before the workshop, and agree the right question with you. [Confirm this with Marcel; it's not yet decided.]

**Timing.** [Elapsed weeks, workshop date, and online or in person.]

**Fee.** [Pilot fee.]

**How we work.** Useful, never indispensable. The aim is a team that can make the next hard decision without us.
