# Five-second tests

**Status:** kit for days 8–10 · **Date:** 8 November 2026 · **Replaces:** strategy 11.2 for this phase

Test whether a stranger can tell, in five seconds, what is offered and who it is for. Run it after the buyer conversations, once the copy uses their words.

## Who

Five to eight people unfamiliar with the practice. Ideally include two or three who match the buyer profile (owner-managers or MDs) and who were not interviewed.

## How

1. Open the page on their own device at the top, with the homepage intro already skipped.
2. Show it for five seconds, then hide it.
3. Ask the three questions below. Write down their exact words.
4. Repeat for each page, in this order: Home, How we help, Workshops & learning.

Keep the design constant. Change only the copy between rounds, so any difference comes from the words.

## The three questions

1. **What does this company do?**
2. **Who is it for?**
3. **What would you click next?**

## What counts as understood

| Page | Understood if they say something like |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Helps leaders or businesses make hard decisions |
| How we help | A one-day workshop, or a short engagement, to settle one decision |
| Workshops & learning | Hands-on workshops and courses, not lectures |

**Target:** most participants match on each page (strategy 12.3).

## Then

- If fewer than half understood a page, rewrite the supporting sentence in the buyers' words from [words.md](words.md) and test again.
- Don't change the headline "More possibilities. Harder choices." on this evidence alone.
