# Booking the conversations: invitations

**Status:** draft for days 3–7 · **Date:** 8 November 2026 · **Goes with:** [interview-sheet.md](interview-sheet.md)

Send these personally, from Marcel's own address or phone, never as a mailing. Ask for 30 minutes, offer two times, and don't attach anything. These are research calls, not sales calls.

## Who to ask first

Warm contacts before cold: people who already know Marcel, or who are one introduction away. A warm "yes" this week beats a perfect profile next month.

Check each person against the profile: owner-manager or MD, a South African business of roughly 20–250 people, and a consequential decision in the last year or the next 90 days.

## Email (warm contact)

> **Subject:** 30 minutes on how you make the big calls?
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm talking to a handful of owners and MDs about how they make the decisions that really matter to the business: what makes them hard, and what helps. I'm not selling anything on this call. I'm trying to understand the problem properly before I build anything around it.
>
> Would you have 30 minutes on [day, time] or [day, time]? A phone or video call is fine.
>
> Thanks,
> Marcel

## Message (WhatsApp or LinkedIn)

> Hi [name], I'm speaking to a few owners and MDs about how they make the big calls in their business. It's research, not a pitch. Could I have 30 minutes of your time next week? [day] or [day] works for me.

## Asking for an introduction

> Hi [name], I'm trying to speak to a few owners or MDs of businesses with roughly 20 to 250 people who've had a hard decision to make recently. It's a 30-minute research conversation, not a pitch. Is there someone you'd be comfortable introducing me to?

## After the call

> Thank you for your time today. It helped a lot. If anything you said turns up on our website, it will be anonymous, and I'll ask you first before quoting you, even without your name.

If they show a buying signal (see the interview sheet), don't pitch on the call. Ask: *"Would it be useful if I sent you a note on how we'd approach that?"* Then send the [Sprint brief](../sales/sprint-brief.md).
