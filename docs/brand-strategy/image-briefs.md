# Image briefs

**Status:** recorded 8 November 2026 · **Implements:** [BRAND_STRATEGY.md](BRAND_STRATEGY.md) 8.4 and 12.3

**Order of preference:** a real photograph first, then a photograph we take ourselves, then a generated image. A prompt is a fallback only.

**Rules for every image:**
- A generated image is captioned "Illustration, AI-generated". It is never shown as client work or as a photograph (strategy 8.4).
- No apes except the logo.
- Palette: ink, white and one touch of yellow.
- No clichés: chess, lightbulbs, puzzle pieces, sticky-note walls, handshakes or robots.
- Before publishing, check the licence and record it in `CLEARANCES.md`.

Briefs 1, 2 and 4 are still lifes without people, so they can't imply clients. They're better photographed than generated.

## 1. Decision record (Sprint section, record page)

Prefer a photograph of the real PDF printout (`public/downloads/sample-decision-record.pdf`).

> Overhead photograph of a single printed A4 page on a pale wooden table, a clean typeset document with a small table of three options and short labelled rows, one row underlined with a yellow highlighter, a black pen resting beside it, soft daylight from a window, shallow depth of field, minimal, editorial, muted colours, no hands, no legible brand names.

*Avoid:* laptops, coffee-cup clichés, clutter, a glossy stock look.

## 2. Strategic Decision Design ("Twelve priorities is another word for none.")

> Close-up photograph of a handwritten list of twelve items on lined paper, eleven firmly crossed out in black ink, one item circled, a pencil lying across the page, natural side light, high contrast black and white with the circle faintly yellow, quiet and deliberate mood.

*Avoid:* sticky notes, whiteboards, people.

## 3. Behavioural Design

No prompt. Use the cleared bakery photographs in `public/images/research/`.

## 4. Human–AI Decision Design ("Who decides")

> Close-up photograph of a hand initialling the margin of a printed page with a fountain pen, a laptop out of focus in the background showing an abstract grey interface, the printed page in sharp focus, daylight, restrained palette of black, white and grey with a small yellow tab on the page edge, calm and serious.

*Avoid:* robots, glowing circuits, brains, blue holograms, visible text on the screen.

## 5. Live workshops (Learning card)

Use this only until a real session photograph with consent exists, then swap it at the first opportunity. Caption: "Illustration, AI-generated".

> Documentary-style photograph from above of a long table during a working session: printed worksheets, a spreadsheet printout with handwritten notes, several pairs of hands mid-gesture pointing at the same page, mugs, natural light, candid not posed, faces not visible, warm but neutral tones.

*Avoid:* glass boardrooms, suits pointing at screens, staged smiles, sticky-note walls.

## 6. Digital courses (Learning card)

Keep the type frame "Make the expensive mistake somewhere cheap." If an image is wanted:

> Photograph of a printed financial case brief and a calculator on a desk beside an open spreadsheet on a laptop, a single cell highlighted in yellow, one sticky tab on the brief, daylight, minimal, sharp focus on the paper.
